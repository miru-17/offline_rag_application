# RAGENTIS – User guide

RAGENTIS answers questions from your company's own documents. It runs on a
computer inside your office; nothing is sent to the internet. It answers only
from the documents you are allowed to see, and shows where each answer came
from.

---

## Sign in

Open the address your IT team gave you (for example `https://ragentis.corp.local:8443`).

* **Company (Windows) account:** if your IT team connected Active Directory, use
  your normal Windows user name and password.
* **Temporary password:** after a reset you must choose your own password first.
* **Two-step login (recommended):** Settings → *Two-step login* → scan the QR
  code with Google or Microsoft Authenticator → enter the 6-digit code. From then
  on you enter a code from the app after your password.

---

## Ask questions – AI Workspace

1. Type your question in plain language, e.g. *"How many days of annual leave do
   I get?"* or *"What is the total of invoice INV-7781?"*
2. The answer appears word by word. **Stop** ends it early.
3. Under the answer, **Evidence** shows each source: file, page, how well it
   matched and the exact passage. **Open at page N** opens the original PDF at
   that page.
4. 👍 / 👎 tells the administrator whether the answer helped.

Tips

* Mention the document if you know it: *"In the travel policy, what is the hotel limit?"*
* Exact codes and numbers (invoice numbers, employee IDs) are found word for word.
* Short follow-ups work: *"explain that more"*, *"and for managers?"*
* If the answer is not in any document you can see, RAGENTIS says so instead of
  guessing.
* You can ask in Tamil, Hindi or another language if your administrator turned
  that on.

---

## Documents

* **Upload:** drag files into the Documents page – PDF, Word, Excel or photos
  of paper documents (PNG/JPG). Scanned pages are read automatically (OCR).
* **Who can see it:** *Private* (only you), a *Team space* (e.g. HR), or the
  whole organisation (administrators).
* **Status:** Queued → Processing → Ready. *Failed* shows the reason and a Retry button.
* **New version:** open the document → *Upload new version*. Answers then use the
  new version; the old one stays in the history.
* **Tables into charts:** for Word and Excel files, open the document → *Find
  tables for the Data Dashboard* → *Open in dashboard*.

---

## Other tools

| Page | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| Data Dashboard | Upload CSV/Excel → totals, monthly trends, charts; ask the data in plain English |
| Extraction | Pull the same fields (invoice number, date, amount…) from many documents into one table |
| Compare | See exactly what changed between two versions of a document |
| Team spaces | Documents shared only with a team such as HR or Finance |

---

## Privacy

* You only ever get answers from documents you are allowed to read.
* Personal data such as Aadhaar or PAN numbers can be hidden from the AI by your
  administrator.
* The audit log records actions (sign-in, upload, delete), not your questions or
  the answers.
